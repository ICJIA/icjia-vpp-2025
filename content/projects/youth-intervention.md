## Overview

Youth intervention programs aim to prevent violence by addressing risk factors and promoting protective factors among young people. These programs work with at-risk youth to provide support, education, and opportunities that help them avoid involvement in violence.

## Key Initiatives

- Early childhood education and family support
- School-based violence prevention programs
- Mentoring and after-school activities
- Youth employment and skill development
- Trauma-informed care and mental health services

## Goals

1. Reduce youth involvement in violence
2. Increase protective factors for at-risk youth
3. Provide positive alternatives to risky behaviors
4. Support families in creating safe and nurturing environments
5. Build community capacity to support youth development
