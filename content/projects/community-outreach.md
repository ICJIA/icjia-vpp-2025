This page contains information about community outreach programs and initiatives as part of the Violence Prevention Plan for Illinois: 2025-2029.

Community outreach programs are essential components of violence prevention efforts. These programs engage directly with community members to build trust, provide resources, and develop collaborative solutions to address violence.

The Violence Prevention Plan for Illinois recognizes that effective community engagement is a cornerstone of successful violence prevention. By working directly with communities most affected by violence, we can develop more responsive and effective interventions that address the root causes of violence. Our approach emphasizes collaboration with existing community organizations, leaders, and residents to ensure that violence prevention efforts are culturally appropriate, accessible, and responsive to local needs and priorities. ## Key Initiatives

- Community violence intervention teams
- Neighborhood safety coalitions
- Faith-based partnerships
- Street outreach and violence interruption
- Community healing and trauma support

## Goals

1. Build stronger relationships between communities and service providers
2. Increase community participation in violence prevention efforts
3. Provide accessible resources and support services
4. Address social determinants of violence
5. Promote community healing and resilience
