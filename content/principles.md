Five core principles guide our violence prevention efforts, ensuring that our work is grounded in equity, safety, health, belonging, and collaboration across all levels of government.


## Foster Belonging and Social Connectedness

Individuals, families, and communities can find acceptance and develop resiliency through healthy, peaceful relationships. Violence prevention programming includes relational opportunities based on developmental stages and risk factors.

### Key Elements
- **Community Building**: Creating spaces where individuals feel valued and connected
- **Relationship Development**: Supporting healthy relationships across all age groups
- **Resiliency Training**: Building capacity to overcome challenges and trauma
- **Developmental Approaches**: Tailoring interventions to specific life stages

### Implementation Strategies
- Peer support programs and mentorship opportunities
- Community events that foster social connections
- Family engagement initiatives
- Youth development programs focused on positive relationships


## Advance Equity

Violence prevention programming addresses inequities by partnering with individuals, families and communities who experience a disproportionate amount of risk factors. State agencies address historical inequities by embedding communities' true voice in decision-making processes.

### Key Elements
- **Community Partnership**: Authentic collaboration with affected communities
- **Historical Awareness**: Acknowledging and addressing past inequities
- **Voice and Choice**: Ensuring community input drives decision-making
- **Resource Distribution**: Prioritizing communities with greatest need

### Implementation Strategies
- Community-led advisory boards and committees
- Culturally responsive programming and services
- Equitable funding distribution mechanisms
- Training on implicit bias and cultural competency

**Related Strategic Priority**: [Strategic Priority #2: Advance Equity](/strategic-priorities#advance-equity)

## Promote Safety

The ability to live without fear of harm is a fundamental human right and developmentally essential to individual, familial and community success. Violence prevention programming strives to develop culturally responsive, safe, and peaceful environments.

### Key Elements
- **Physical Safety**: Creating secure environments free from violence
- **Emotional Safety**: Supporting mental health and psychological well-being
- **Cultural Responsiveness**: Respecting diverse community values and practices
- **Environmental Design**: Using space and place to promote safety

### Implementation Strategies
- Environmental design improvements (lighting, landscaping, etc.)
- Conflict resolution and mediation programs
- Mental health and trauma services
- Community safety planning initiatives

**Related Strategic Priority**: [Strategic Priority #1: Prevent Violence & Promote Safety](/strategic-priorities#prevent-violence-promote-safety)


## Support Health

Violence prevention programming builds and sustains mentally and physically strong individuals, families, and communities. These trauma-informed practices include policies and efforts that support staff in local organizations.

### Key Elements
- **Holistic Wellness**: Addressing physical, mental, and emotional health
- **Trauma-Informed Care**: Understanding and responding to trauma impacts
- **Staff Support**: Ensuring organizational wellness and sustainability
- **Community Health**: Building healthy environments and systems

### Implementation Strategies
- Comprehensive health and wellness programs
- Trauma-informed training for all staff and volunteers
- Employee assistance programs and self-care initiatives
- Community health assessments and improvement planning

**Related Strategic Priority**: [Strategic Priority #1: Prevent Violence & Promote Safety](/strategic-priorities#prevent-violence-promote-safety)

## Engage State Agencies in Collaboration

Violence prevention programming takes place across many state agencies and focuses on similar outcomes. We work more effectively by communicating across agencies quarterly, sharing resources, best practices and data as the norm.

### Key Elements
- **Inter-Agency Coordination**: Regular communication and planning across departments
- **Resource Sharing**: Maximizing efficiency through shared tools and knowledge
- **Data Integration**: Coordinated data collection and analysis
- **Best Practice Exchange**: Learning from successful initiatives statewide

### Implementation Strategies
- Quarterly inter-agency coordination meetings
- Shared data systems and reporting mechanisms
- Joint training and professional development opportunities
- Collaborative funding and grant opportunities

**Related Strategic Priority**: [Strategic Priority #3: Promote Collaboration](/strategic-priorities#promote-collaboration)


## How Principles Guide Our Work

These five principles work together to create a comprehensive framework for violence prevention in Illinois:

1. **Foundation**: Principles provide the values-based foundation for all activities
2. **Decision-Making**: Every program and policy decision is evaluated against these principles
3. **Accountability**: Regular assessment ensures alignment with principle-based goals
4. **Continuous Improvement**: Principles guide adaptation and enhancement of efforts

### Integration with Strategic Priorities

Our guiding principles directly inform and support our [Strategic Priorities: 2025-2029](/strategic-priorities), creating a cohesive approach to violence prevention that is both values-driven and action-oriented.
