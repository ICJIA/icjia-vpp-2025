Our violence prevention plan is built around three core goals that address the full spectrum of prevention, equity, and collaboration needed to create lasting change in Illinois communities.


## Goal #1: Prevent Violence & Promote Safety

Implement evidence-based prevention strategies through trauma-informed/healing-centered approaches that address primary, secondary, and tertiary prevention across all community levels.

### Strategic Focus Areas

#### Honor Complex Histories and Provide Holistic Services
- Acknowledge historical trauma and its ongoing impacts
- Develop comprehensive service models that address root causes
- Integrate cultural healing practices and community wisdom
- Support multi-generational approaches to healing

#### Engage Families, Schools, and Communities
- Strengthen family support systems and parenting resources
- Implement school-based prevention and intervention programs
- Build community capacity for violence prevention
- Foster partnerships between institutions and communities

#### Address Multiple Risk and Protective Factors
- Target individual, relationship, community, and societal factors
- Strengthen protective factors while reducing risk exposure
- Use comprehensive assessment tools to guide interventions
- Implement coordinated service delivery models

#### Focus on Groups at Higher Risk for Violence
- Prioritize communities with highest violence rates
- Address disparities in violence exposure and outcomes
- Develop targeted interventions for high-risk populations
- Ensure culturally appropriate and accessible services

#### Use Evidence-Based Practices
- Implement programs with demonstrated effectiveness
- Adapt evidence-based models to local contexts
- Conduct ongoing evaluation and quality improvement
- Share lessons learned and best practices statewide

### Related Guiding Principles
This strategic priority is directly supported by our principles of [Promote Safety](/principles#promote-safety) and [Support Health](/principles#support-health).

## Goal #2: Advance Equity

Increase access to grants and economic opportunities through flexible funding that accommodates local community priorities and addresses historical inequities.

### Strategic Focus Areas

#### Continue Improving Access to Funding
- Streamline application and reporting processes
- Provide technical assistance for grant applications
- Reduce barriers for small and emerging organizations
- Expand funding opportunities for community-led initiatives

#### Support Small and Medium-Sized Organizations
- Offer capacity building and organizational development support
- Create mentorship programs connecting experienced and new organizations
- Provide flexible funding terms that accommodate organizational needs
- Develop partnerships that strengthen smaller organizations

#### Improve Reimbursement and Budget Processes
- Reduce time between service delivery and reimbursement
- Simplify budget reporting and modification procedures
- Provide clear guidance on allowable expenses and activities
- Implement user-friendly financial management systems

#### Promote and Connect Grantee Work
- Showcase successful programs and innovations
- Facilitate networking and collaboration among grantees
- Create platforms for sharing resources and best practices
- Support peer learning and mutual support networks

#### Improve Systems and Communication
- Develop centralized systems for grant management
- Enhance communication channels between funders and grantees
- Provide regular updates on funding opportunities and requirements
- Create feedback mechanisms for continuous improvement

### Related Guiding Principles
This strategic priority directly implements our principle of [Advance Equity](/principles#advance-equity).



## Goal #3: Promote Collaboration

Foster partnerships across state, municipal, and community-based agencies through research-informed data sharing and best practice exchange.

### Strategic Focus Areas

#### Analyze and Disseminate Statewide Data Trends
- Collect and analyze violence prevention data across agencies
- Produce regular reports on violence trends and prevention outcomes
- Share data insights with stakeholders and communities
- Use data to inform policy and program decisions

#### Coordinate Violence Prevention Funding
- Align funding streams across state agencies
- Reduce duplication and maximize resource efficiency
- Create coordinated funding announcements and timelines
- Develop shared evaluation and reporting requirements

#### Continue the Ad Hoc Violence Prevention Committee
- Maintain regular inter-agency coordination meetings
- Expand participation to include municipal and community partners
- Develop shared goals and accountability measures
- Create working groups on specific violence prevention topics

#### Host Statewide Violence Prevention Events
- Organize annual conferences and learning exchanges
- Facilitate regional networking and collaboration opportunities
- Provide training and professional development opportunities
- Celebrate successes and share promising practices

#### Build Community Across Stakeholders
- Foster relationships between diverse violence prevention partners
- Create opportunities for cross-sector collaboration
- Support community-led initiatives and leadership development
- Facilitate dialogue between different perspectives and approaches

### Related Guiding Principles
This strategic priority directly implements our principle of [Engage State Agencies in Collaboration](/principles#engage-state-agencies-in-collaboration).



## Implementation Framework

### Timeline and Milestones
Each strategic priority includes specific milestones and deliverables across the 2025-2029 planning period, with annual progress reviews and adjustments.

### Measurement and Evaluation
Success will be measured through:
- Quantitative indicators (violence rates, program reach, funding distribution)
- Qualitative assessments (stakeholder feedback, community engagement)
- Process evaluations (implementation fidelity, partnership effectiveness)
- Outcome evaluations (long-term impact on violence prevention)

### Accountability and Oversight
The Ad Hoc Violence Prevention Committee provides ongoing oversight and accountability for strategic priority implementation, with regular reporting to stakeholders and communities.



## Connection to Guiding Principles

These strategic priorities are grounded in and guided by our [Five Guiding Principles](/principles), ensuring that all implementation efforts remain true to our core values while achieving measurable outcomes.
