# Illinois Launches Statewide Violence Prevention Data Sharing Initiative

The Illinois Criminal Justice Information Authority, in partnership with the University of Illinois and multiple state agencies, today launched the Illinois Violence Prevention Data Collaborative - a groundbreaking initiative to improve data sharing and coordination across violence prevention efforts statewide.

## Platform Overview

The new data sharing platform provides:

- **Real-time violence trend monitoring** across all 102 Illinois counties
- **Secure data sharing** between authorized agencies and organizations
- **Interactive dashboards** for community-level violence prevention planning
- **Research collaboration tools** for academic and community partners

## Participating Agencies

The collaborative includes data from:

### State Agencies
- Illinois Department of Public Health
- Illinois State Police
- Illinois Department of Children and Family Services
- Illinois Department of Human Services
- Illinois Department of Corrections

### Local Partners
- Chicago Police Department
- Cook County Health System
- Major hospital systems statewide
- Community-based violence prevention organizations

### Academic Institutions
- University of Illinois Violence Prevention Research Lab
- Northwestern University
- University of Chicago Crime Lab
- Southern Illinois University

## Data Types and Sources

The platform integrates multiple data sources while maintaining strict privacy protections:

### Health Data
- Emergency department visits for violence-related injuries
- Mental health service utilization
- Substance abuse treatment admissions
- Trauma-informed care program participation

### Criminal Justice Data
- Incident reports and arrest data
- Court case outcomes
- Probation and parole supervision
- Recidivism tracking

### Community Data
- Violence prevention program participation
- Educational outcomes and school climate
- Employment and housing stability
- Community asset mapping

## Privacy and Security

The platform implements robust privacy protections:

- **De-identification** of all personal information
- **Aggregate reporting** to prevent individual identification
- **Role-based access** controls for different user types
- **Regular security audits** and compliance monitoring

## Use Cases and Applications

### Community Planning
Local organizations can access neighborhood-level data to:
- Identify violence hotspots and trends
- Plan targeted intervention programs
- Measure program effectiveness
- Coordinate resources across organizations

### Research and Evaluation
Researchers can use the platform to:
- Conduct longitudinal studies on violence prevention
- Evaluate program effectiveness across multiple sites
- Identify risk and protective factors
- Develop predictive models for violence prevention

### Policy Development
State and local policymakers can:
- Track progress toward violence reduction goals
- Identify funding priorities and gaps
- Develop evidence-based policy recommendations
- Monitor implementation of violence prevention initiatives

## Training and Technical Assistance

The initiative includes comprehensive support for users:

### Training Programs
- Monthly webinars on platform features
- Quarterly in-person training sessions
- Customized training for specific user groups
- Online resource library and tutorials

### Technical Assistance
- Dedicated help desk for user support
- Data analysis consultation services
- Custom report development
- Integration support for local data systems

## Early Results and Impact

Since the pilot launch in July 2024, the platform has:

- **Connected 45 organizations** across 12 counties
- **Generated 150+ custom reports** for community planning
- **Supported 8 research studies** on violence prevention effectiveness
- **Informed $1.2 million** in targeted funding decisions

## Future Enhancements

Planned improvements include:

## Getting Access

Organizations interested in joining the collaborative can:

1. **Complete the application** at [data.illinoisvpp.org](https://data.illinoisvpp.org)
2. **Attend a training session** on data use and privacy requirements
3. **Sign data sharing agreements** with participating agencies
4. **Begin accessing** platform features and resources

## Measuring Success

The initiative will be evaluated based on:

- Number of participating organizations and data sources
- Frequency and quality of data use for decision-making
- Evidence of improved coordination between agencies
- Measurable improvements in violence prevention outcomes

This data sharing initiative represents a critical component of Illinois' commitment to evidence-based violence prevention. Learn more about our [collaborative approach](/about) and [strategic priorities](/strategic-priorities).
