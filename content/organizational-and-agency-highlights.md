Highlighting key organizations and agencies that are making significant contributions to violence prevention efforts across Illinois.

## Key Organizations and Agencies

### ICJIA – Institute 2 Innovate (I2I)

The Illinois Criminal Justice Information Authority's Institute 2 Innovate (I2I) program focuses on developing innovative approaches to violence prevention and criminal justice reform.

**Learn more:** [Institute 2 Innovate](https://icjia.illinois.gov/i2i)

### Government Alliance for Safe Communities (GASC)

The Government Alliance for Safe Communities works to reduce violence through evidence-based strategies and community partnerships.

**Learn more:** [Government Alliance for Safe Communities](https://www.gasc.org)

### ICADV – Survivor Focus Groups

The Illinois Coalition Against Domestic Violence (ICADV) conducts survivor focus groups to ensure that the voices and experiences of survivors inform violence prevention strategies.

**Learn more:** [Illinois Coalition Against Domestic Violence](https://www.ilcadv.org)

### IDPH – Office for Violence Prevention (OFVP)

The Illinois Department of Public Health's Office for Violence Prevention leads statewide efforts to prevent violence through public health approaches.

**Learn more:** [IDPH Office for Violence Prevention](https://dph.illinois.gov/topics-services/prevention-wellness/injury-violence-prevention.html)

### ICJIA Grantee Highlights

The Illinois Criminal Justice Information Authority supports numerous grantees across the state who are implementing innovative violence prevention programs in their communities.

**Learn more:** [ICJIA Grant Programs](https://icjia.illinois.gov/grants)
